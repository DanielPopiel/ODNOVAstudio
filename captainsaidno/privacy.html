Privacy Policy – Captain Said No

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Captain Said No is an entertainment and education brand about aviation, run by Daniel Popiel (Odnova Studio, Poland). Its characters are AI-generated. This policy describes how the publishing tool used for the brand's own accounts handles data.

What the tool does

The tool publishes the brand's own videos to the brand's own accounts on Instagram, YouTube and TikTok, on a schedule. It is used only by the account owner. It is not offered to the public and does not ask other people to log in.

Data we access

When the owner authorizes access, the tool receives an access token for the brand's own account and, where the platform allows it, basic account information and the status of its own posts. We do not collect, store or sell personal data of viewers, followers or any other third party.

How data is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in files that are not shared. They are used only to publish and to read the status of the brand's own posts. The owner can revoke access at any time in the settings of each platform, after which the tool can no longer act on the account.

Sharing

We do not share data with third parties, other than with the platforms (Instagram, YouTube, TikTok) as needed to publish the owner's content.

Children

The tool is not directed at children and does not collect data from children.

Contact

Questions about this policy: send a message to @captainsaidno on Instagram.
