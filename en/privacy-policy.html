Privacy policy

Below I explain what data I collect on odnovastudio.com, why, how long I keep it and what your rights are. The website does not use analytics tools or tracking cookies.

1. Who is the data controller

The data controller is Daniel Popiel, running a sole proprietorship under the ODNOVA°studio brand, VAT ID PL8442349301. For any matter concerning personal data, write to kontakt@odnovastudio.com.

2. What data I collect and why

  • The “Prepare 3 versions” form — your website address and email address. I use them to prepare website proposals and reply to your request (legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR — steps taken at your request before entering into a contract).
  • Email correspondence — the data you provide in your message. I use it to talk with you, prepare a quote and deliver the project (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), and afterwards to establish or defend possible claims (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Accounting records — if we work together, I keep the data needed for invoicing for as long as tax law requires (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

Providing data is voluntary, but without an email address I can’t send you the proposals.

3. Who I share data with

I don’t sell data or share it for marketing. I use external providers who process data on my behalf:

  • Web3Forms — forwards the form contents to my mailbox,
  • Google (Gmail, Google Workspace) — email,
  • GitHub (GitHub Pages) — website hosting; the server may keep technical logs, including IP addresses,
  • Google Fonts — the typefaces are loaded from Google’s servers, which see your device’s IP address.

Some of these companies are based outside the European Economic Area (USA). Transfers rely on the European Commission’s EU–US Data Privacy Framework decision or on standard contractual clauses.

4. How long I keep data

  • enquiries that didn’t lead to a project — up to 12 months after the last contact,
  • correspondence related to a project — for the duration of our cooperation and until the limitation period for claims expires,
  • accounting records — for the period required by law (usually 5 years from the end of the tax year).

5. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict processing, to data portability and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. Just write to kontakt@odnovastudio.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Polish data protection authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw), or with the authority in your country.

I don’t make automated decisions about you or profile your data.

6. Cookies and browser storage

The website sets no cookies. It only uses the browser’s session storage (sessionStorage) so the intro animation isn’t shown again during the same visit. This contains no personal data and disappears when you close the tab.

7. Demo websites

The sample websites shown in the portfolio (e.g. a law firm, a car repair shop, a guesthouse) present fictional businesses, and the contact details on them are placeholders.

8. Changes to this policy

If the way I process data changes, I’ll update this page and the date below.
